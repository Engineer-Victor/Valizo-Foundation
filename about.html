Vision and mission

Guided by a clear commitment to lasting community impact.

✦

Our Vision

To foster a world where children and young people living in Uganda and communities have access to medical services and information, life skills, education opportunities and support needed to thrive and lead healthy, productive lives.

♥

Our Mission

To empower Ugandan children and communities through creating access to medical services, health information and education opportunities that foster lasting growth and sustainable development.

+

Our Approach

We work through community engagement, partnerships, advocacy, awareness, care programs and practical services that respond to real needs.

⚖

Our Promise

To serve vulnerable people with dignity, transparency and accountability while building solutions that can create long term change.

Our Story

Born from lived experience, built for community transformation.

Valizo Foundation was created from a deep understanding of the challenges faced by vulnerable children, young people and underserved families in Uganda.

Our work focuses on practical care, access to health information, education support, protection, advocacy and opportunities that help children and communities move forward with dignity.

Our Values
