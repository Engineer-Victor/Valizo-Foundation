Every child deserves safety, learning and love.

Valizo Foundation supports vulnerable children and young people in hard-to-reach communities by providing assistance in basic needs, education, healthcare, advocacy, psychosocial support, and family strengthening. The foundation is inspired by the lived experiences of vulnerable children in Uganda, including those of its founders, and is driven by a commitment to creating an environment where every child can thrive. Through its work, Valizo Foundation seeks to ensure that vulnerable children have access to the health, educational, and livelihood opportunities they need to realize their full potential.
